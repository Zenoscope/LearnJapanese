menu system 
 Uses an breadcrumbs array to track which room you are in, clicking "back" 
 removes that entry from the array.
 The room name and the button (minus the btn_ part) are used to navigate it.

kana grid
 Usees the kana arrays to populate the grid.
 Kana buttons change the kana_array[0] or [1] or [2] to change from yoon or whatever.
  The button refreshes the grid.
 The buttons flip the cards (sprite animation) and change the face from kana to romanji
  and play the sound
